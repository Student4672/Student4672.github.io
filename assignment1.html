People Sitting on the Streets of Ginza Spark Debate

Visitors Need Places to Rest

Ginza is a popular shopping area in Tokyo. Recently, some visitors have been sitting on the ground because they cannot find a place to rest.

Some tourists walk for a long time and become tired. Cafes and restaurants may be full, and there are not enough benches in the area.

A Visitor's Opinion

A visitor from Malaysia said that she had walked for about an hour after visiting Osaka. She explained: I would be happy if there were more benches where I could take a break.

Having more places to sit could make visiting Ginza more comfortable for tourists.

Why Are There So Few Benches?

According to the police, adding benches is not easy. Ginza cares about its city image and the appearance of its streets. There is also limited space.

Sitting on the street is not automatically illegal. However, it may become a problem if people block pedestrians or interfere with store businesses. Everyone needs to use public spaces safely and considerately.
